<h3 align="center">myGeeKy: your corner of GitHub, for research</h3>

<p align="center">
A small panel at the edge of your screen that finds researchers who work on what you work on,<br>
projects in your field that welcome help, and the papers, models and news worth your time.
</p>

---

**Safe by design.** myGeeKy only reads public GitHub data. It never follows, stars or posts for you: it suggests, and you decide.
Signing in with GitHub gives it no extra access to your account, and your token stays on your own computer or phone.

**Install:** `pip install mygeeky`, the [Windows installer](https://github.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/releases/latest),
or the one-line Linux install on [mygeeky.org](https://mygeeky.org).
